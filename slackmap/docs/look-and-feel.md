# Look and feel

SlackMap is a platform for the community. It is a volunteer project, not a corporate one: it needs to be sincere and warm rather than official looking, friendly with some playful vibes. Above all it must look made by a single person with one coherent mind. Playful must not mean old-school websites made to be playful, and the spacing stays compact, not roomy.

## How it holds together

- **One theme.** A screen uses the theme's parts plainly instead of painting its own, so the look cannot drift apart.
- **The map is the page.** The side panel, the cards, the forms and the pop-ups float on it. The blue panel is the one colored surface; everything you read, fill in or decide on is white.
- **Colors have jobs.** Blue is the main action, one per view. Magenta means activated, and only that. Red is delete, and a line on the map; green is success, and a spot.
- **Friendly by detail, not by cartoon.** Icons and the active dot carry the friendliness. Flat drawings are for pictures that explain or stand in for something (the activation's how-it-works, the tutorial, a missing photo), and there is no emoji.

## Decided against the defaults

- Titles are plain Barlow, not the ISA brand's Barlow Condensed: a deliberate SlackMap exception to the brand.
- No dark mode for now.
