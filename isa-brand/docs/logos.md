# Logo use

From the ISA CD Manual, with the screen sizes and clear space set for ISA web apps.

1. Use the files in [logos](/isa-brand/logos/) as they are: never redraw, recolor, outline, add effects, stretch, squeeze, rotate or crop. Resize only in proportion.
2. Use the color variant. On green, red, dark or busy backgrounds, and on `--isa-color-surface-brand`, use white. Black or grey only where color is not possible.
3. `isa-default` where there is room; `isa-wide` in headers and other wide, low places; `isa-minimal` at small sizes, as icon, avatar or favicon, and never as the only ISA logo on something for people outside the ISA.
4. Minimum height on screen: `isa-default` 64px, `isa-wide` 32px, `isa-minimal` 24px (16px as favicon or app icon), commission logos 48px, certification marks 24px. Below that, switch to `isa-minimal`. In print, 16mm.
5. Clear space on all sides of at least half the height of the ISA mark (the red and green shape). No text, pictures, edges or other logos inside it.
6. Never combine a logo with other graphics into a new mark, use it as a button label or inside a sentence.
7. Alt text names the ISA (and "home" when it links there); a logo next to the same name in text gets `alt=""`.
8. The ISA name and logos are not under the MIT licence ([LICENSE](/isa-brand/LICENSE)). ISA and its commissions use them freely; members and partners need permission.
